I’ve already noted what “a few more days” would mean to the luckless troops and sailors
on the spot, and as to being thoughtful when “opening up the age of nuclear warfare,” of
course no one was focusing on anything as portentous as that, which reflects a
historian’s tidy hindsight. The U.S. government was engaged not in that sort of

momentous thing but in ending the war conclusively, as well as irrationally
remembering Pearl Harbor with a vengeance.
